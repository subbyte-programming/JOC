Daniels/Devlin House

Architecture / Modernist Residence

Material richness and crisp geometry in one quiet composition.

Daniels/Devlin House was approached as a study in balance — where crisp architectural forms meet softened natural light and deeply tactile surfaces. The work highlights how structure, rhythm, and landscape can coexist without taking over the serenity of the home.

The final set is intentionally restrained but expressive, drawing attention to the way every window, mullion, and plane contributes to the feeling of openness and calm.
