Art & Architecture

Architecture / Art Direction

Where form, light, and material conversation become a visual story.

Art & Architecture explores the crossover between built form and curated interiors, where each object and surface contributes to a broader narrative. The visual language is grounded in quiet contrast, volume, and a confidence in negative space.

Each scene is arranged to celebrate both structure and detail without over-scaling the frame. The result is a refined, editorial set that speaks to the authority of architecture and the softness of lived-in interiors.
