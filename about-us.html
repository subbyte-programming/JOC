About

JOC

Capturing architecture with atmosphere and clarity.

Jeannie O'Connor is an architectural photographer known for creating evocative, honest images that reveal how light, texture, and material shape a space. Her work balances precision with feeling, turning modern homes and intimate interiors into stories that feel both tactile and timeless.

From California estates to homes abroad, each commission begins with close observation. The goal is not simply a record of architecture, but a natural rendering that preserves the spirit of the project, the details that make it unique, and the lived experience within it.

Her portfolio spans residential architecture, renovations, landscape integration, and art-centered interiors. With a collaborative approach and attention to atmosphere, each image is designed to reflect the intent of the architect, client, and place.
