Aidells/Oakes Healdsburg House

Architecture / Wine Country Living

Quiet luxury, grounded in landscape and ritual.

The Healdsburg residence was photographed to emphasize the relationship between the home and the land around it. The composition uses long, calm views and architectural detail to show how simplicity and material richness can work in harmony.

Every image is designed to feel grounded, tactile, and rooted in place — a clear portrait of life in the Sonoma countryside.
