Featured project

Southampton

Architecture / Residence

Calm, gallery-like interiors shaped by warm natural light.

Southampton was photographed to emphasize the relationship between architecture and landscape, with a soft editorial rhythm that lets the room breathe. The emphasis is on proportion, material nuance, and the kind of light that makes a modern home feel intimate and timeless.

Every frame was composed to preserve the sense of openness while still revealing the tactile qualities of stone, timber, and glass. The result feels elegantly resolved and deeply grounded in place.
