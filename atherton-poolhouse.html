Atherton Poolhouse

Architecture / Outdoor Living

Outdoor luxury framed with softness, geometry, and stillness.

Atherton Poolhouse was photographed with a focus on rhythm and atmospheric calm. The structure’s sculptural geometry and shaded materials are rendered with a subtle cinematic softness that makes the setting feel both refined and restful.

Rather than simply documenting the form, the work celebrates the way the house holds light, the texture of stone under evening glow, and the slow calm of California outdoor living.
