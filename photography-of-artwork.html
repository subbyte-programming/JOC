Photography of Artwork

Architecture / Artwork

Lighting, scale, and material always create the atmosphere.

Photography of Artwork addresses the subtle but critical relationship between object, backdrop, and the way light reveals form. The goal is never to overpower the work, but to create a quiet context that allows the piece and the space to feel fully in conversation.

These frames maintain the exacting balance of composition and atmosphere that defines JOC’s architectural and editorial practice: elegant, atmospheric, and intimately observant.
